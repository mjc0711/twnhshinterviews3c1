国际移工与跨文化经验｜采访资料整理

【教育工作者】陈林月（中国）

采访人：俪恩、盈盈、巧双、铃敏、钰祺

陈林月来自中国湖北，于2005年来到马来西亚求学。大学毕业后，他曾回到中国工作一段时间，但在比较两地的生活与工作环境后，发现自己更适应马来西亚的生活节奏与社会氛围。他指出，中国的工作压力相对较大，而马来西亚的环境较为平衡，也更符合他的个性与价值观，因此最终选择留在马来西亚发展。在跨文化适应方面，他认为最大的挑战来自沟通方式与处理事情的文化差异，需要时间慢慢理解与调整。在教育理念上，他观察到马来西亚较重视多元文化与学生兴趣的发展，而中国虽然逐渐改革，但整体仍以考试制度为核心。

Chen Linyue is from Hubei, China, and came to Malaysia in 2005 for his studies. After graduating, he returned to China to work for a period of time. However, after comparing the living and working environments of both countries, he found that he was more suited to the pace of life and social atmosphere in Malaysia. He pointed out that work pressure in China is relatively high, while the environment in Malaysia is more balanced and better aligns with his personality and values, which led him to choose to continue his career in Malaysia. In terms of cross-cultural adaptation, he believes that the main challenge lies in differences in communication styles and ways of handling matters, which require time to adjust to. Regarding educational philosophy, he observed that Malaysia places greater emphasis on multiculturalism and the development of students’ interests, while China, despite gradual reforms, remains largely examination-oriented.

【外籍移工】Santosh（尼泊尔）

采访人：慈恩、陈愿、靖萱、歆涵

Santosh 来自尼泊尔，在马来西亚担任保安已有五年。他每天工作时间长达十二小时，体力消耗大，但仍努力坚持。他与同事和上司关系良好，也接受过相关安全培训。虽然没有固定休息日，但加班可获得额外补贴。初到马来西亚时，语言尤其是英语是他最大的挑战，但他每天都会与家人联系，并计划在工作准证到期后返回尼泊尔。

Santosh is from Nepal and has worked as a security guard in Malaysia for five years. He works up to twelve hours a day, which is physically demanding, yet he continues to persevere. He maintains good relationships with his colleagues and supervisors and has received relevant safety training. Although he does not have fixed rest days, overtime work is compensated. When he first arrived in Malaysia, language—especially English—was his biggest challenge, but he contacts his family daily and plans to return to Nepal after his work permit expires.

【外籍移工】Osamen（缅甸）

采访人：慈恩、陈愿、靖萱、歆涵

Osamen 来自缅甸，自2008年来到马来西亚，在本地菜市场工作已接近十八年，主要从事切菜等体力劳动。他表示对目前的生活状况感到满意，并已在马来西亚成家立业，与妻子和孩子共同生活。只要工作准证允许，他希望能继续留在马来西亚工作。

Osamen is from Myanmar and has been in Malaysia since 2008. He has worked at a local wet market for nearly eighteen years, mainly doing physically demanding tasks such as cutting vegetables. He stated that he is satisfied with his current living conditions and has established a family in Malaysia, living together with his wife and children. As long as his work permit allows, he hopes to continue working in Malaysia.

【外籍移工】Khari（印尼）

采访人：洺崴、仁祥、奇尉、国楊

Khari 来自印尼，在马来西亚从事建筑业已有近八年。他已婚，但妻子和孩子仍留在印尼。由于家乡工作机会有限、收入偏低，他选择来到马来西亚工作，希望为家人提供更稳定的生活。虽然经常思念家人，但他仍选择坚持工作，并期待未来能够回国与家人团聚。

Khari is from Indonesia and has been working in the construction industry in Malaysia for nearly eight years. He is married, but his wife and child remain in Indonesia. Due to limited job opportunities and low wages in his hometown, he chose to work in Malaysia in order to provide a more stable life for his family. Although he often misses his family, he continues to persevere and hopes to reunite with them in the future.

【外籍移工】Sodik（印尼）

采访人：嘉芯、俪璇、珈媃、锶泳

Sodik 来自印尼，在马来西亚工作已八年。他家中有七位兄弟姐妹，出国工作的主要原因是分担家庭经济压力。他表示在马来西亚的生活与工作整体顺利，并未遭遇歧视，但因合约关系，通常三年才能回乡一次。

Sodik is from Indonesia and has worked in Malaysia for eight years. He has seven siblings, and his main reason for working overseas is to help support his family financially. He stated that his life and work in Malaysia have been generally smooth and that he has not experienced discrimination. However, due to contractual arrangements, he is usually only able to return home once every three years.

【外籍移工】Vanessa Dizon（印尼 / 东帝汶）

采访人：恩瑜、凯恩、嘉淇

Vanessa 现年三十三岁，从事女佣工作，在马来西亚已有十五年。她自幼家庭经济困难，高中毕业后因无力升学而选择出国工作。她将大部分收入用于照顾弟弟妹妹的生活与学业，并已购置土地与房屋作为未来的生活保障。她表示自己从未后悔出国工作，并希望在三十五至三十六岁之间存够积蓄后回国。

Vanessa is 33 years old and has worked as a domestic helper in Malaysia for fifteen years. Due to financial difficulties, she was unable to pursue further education after graduating from high school and chose to work overseas. She uses most of her income to support her younger siblings’ living expenses and education, and has purchased land and houses as security for her future. She stated that she has never regretted working abroad and hopes to return home after saving enough money between the ages of 35 and 36.

【外籍移工】茜拉（印尼）

采访人：美珺、乐扬、羡旎、媚心

茜拉现年二十八岁，在马来西亚从事办公室清洁工作已有四年。她已婚，但丈夫与孩子仍留在印尼。她每天都会与家人通电话，即使工作再疲惫也不间断。由于经济限制，她很少外出或旅游。她曾在工作时得知弟弟去世的消息，却无法即时返国，这成为她最难忘也最心痛的经历。

Sheila is 28 years old and has worked as an office cleaner in Malaysia for four years. She is married, but her husband and child remain in Indonesia. She contacts her family by phone every day, no matter how tired she is after work. Due to financial constraints, she rarely goes out or travels. One of her most painful experiences was learning about her brother’s passing while at work, yet being unable to return home immediately.

【外籍移工】Hann Lin Oo（缅甸）

采访人：铃恩、谊璘、军壹、宝瑩

Hann Lin Oo 现年二十岁，于2023年来到马来西亚，在学校四楼咖啡厅工作。由于缅甸国内战争持续，工作机会稀少，他选择冒险出国谋生，并将大部分收入寄回家中。尽管通讯条件有限，他仍努力与家人保持联系，并期望未来国家局势能够改善。

Hann Lin Oo is 20 years old and came to Malaysia in 2023. He works at a café on the fourth floor of a school. Due to ongoing conflict in Myanmar and limited job opportunities, he chose to take the risk of working overseas and sends most of his income back to his family. Despite limited communication conditions, he makes an effort to stay in touch with his family and hopes for an improvement in the situation in his home country.

【外籍移工】Alicia（印尼）

Alicia 已婚并育有两名孩子，在马来西亚工作多年。她将一半收入寄回家庭，一半留作日常生活开销。她每天都会与家人视讯联系，但由于经济与工作限制，已多年未能返乡。她最喜欢的本地食物是 Roti Canai，生活范围多集中在巴生一带。

Alicia is married and has two children. She has worked in Malaysia for many years and sends half of her income back to her family while keeping the rest for daily expenses. She video calls her family every day, but due to financial and work constraints, she has not been able to return home for many years. Her favourite local food is roti canai, and her daily activities are mostly centred around Klang.
